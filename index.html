Just for you

Two letters,
with love.

Open them in your own time. ❤️

My dear little sister,

There is something very special I’ve wanted to tell you, and I wanted you to hear it from me first. ❤️

After eight years of waiting, hoping, and the heartbreak of our failed IVF, I’m still learning to believe that this new chapter is beginning. Some days I feel happy; others, I’m afraid to say it aloud.

You’ve been in my thoughts throughout. I know how much you’ve been carrying, though I can’t know exactly how each day feels for you. I kept wishing I could share my news gently, but in searching for the right moment, I let too many pass.

I’m sorry I kept something so important from you. I understand if my silence hurts. It wasn’t because I didn’t want you close—I was scared, uncertain, and struggling to find the words. You’ve always been one of the first people I want to tell everything to, and keeping this from you felt wrong.

You’re my little sister, and so much of my life feels incomplete until I’ve shared it with you. Please don’t feel you have to set aside your own feelings for me or reply right away. Take all the time you need. I love you just as much in the quiet moments as in the happy ones.

I’m still your Didi. You can tell me when things are difficult, be upset with me, laugh with me, or call to talk about absolutely nothing, just as always.

I wish I could sit beside you and hold your hand while you read this. You are one of the most important people in my life, and I wanted you to hear this from me, with all my love.

With all my love,
Didi ❤️

My dearest Masi,

I’m coming… and I wanted to send you a little hello before we meet. ❤️

For now, I’m very small and staying in Mumma’s tummy. It’s cosy here, although I have a few complaints about the menu. I’ve passed them on. Apparently, Mumma has noticed.

I sent you these tiny socks because I’m not quite ready to put my feet in them yet. For now, they’ll have to do the visiting for me.

There’s a little story behind why my hello took so long.

Mumma and Baba waited eight years for this news. After so much hope and a difficult disappointment, they found themselves smiling and worrying at the same time. Mumma is still learning to say, “There’s a baby,” without feeling afraid.

And she wanted so much to tell you.

But when she tried to write to you, the words didn’t come easily. She loves you, and she wanted to be gentle. She kept waiting until she knew what to say, and then felt sad that she had waited.

She’s sorry for being quiet, Masi. So she and Baba have helped me send this little letter.

Well, Baba did the typing. I’m currently quite busy growing fingers.

I don’t know much about the world yet, but I’ve heard I have a Masi in it. Someone who knew my Mumma long before she was my Mumma. Someone who can tell me what she was like when she was little—and whether she always liked being in charge.

Please save those stories for me. Especially the ones Mumma might conveniently forget.

One day, I hope these little feet will be resting in your lap while you tell me one. Perhaps I’ll hold your finger and fall asleep halfway through. Please don’t take it personally; I’ll be new at listening.

Until then, there’s no hurry to write back. This box is simply a little hello, some sweetness, and a lot of love from our home to yours.

With the tiniest hug,
Your little niece or nephew ❤️

P.S. The sweets are for you. My present food arrangements are already taken care of.

With all our love ♡
