Just for you

Two letters,
with love.

Open them in your own time. ❤️

My dear little sister,

There is something very special I’ve wanted to tell you, and I wanted you to hear it from me first. ❤️

After eight years of waiting, hoping, and the heartbreak of our failed IVF, I’m still learning to believe that this new chapter is beginning. Some days I feel happy; others, I’m afraid to say it aloud.

You’ve been in my thoughts throughout. I know how much you’ve been carrying, though I can’t know exactly how each day feels for you. I kept wishing I could share my news gently, but in searching for the right moment, I let too many pass.

I’m sorry I kept something so important from you. I understand if my silence hurts. It wasn’t because I didn’t want you close—I was scared, uncertain, and struggling to find the words. You’ve always been one of the first people I want to tell everything to, and keeping this from you felt wrong.

You’re my little sister, and so much of my life feels incomplete until I’ve shared it with you. Please don’t feel you have to set aside your own feelings for me or reply right away. Take all the time you need. I love you just as much in the quiet moments as in the happy ones.

I’m still your Didi. You can tell me when things are difficult, be upset with me, laugh with me, or call to talk about absolutely nothing, just as always.

I wish I could sit beside you and hold your hand while you read this. You are one of the most important people in my life, and I wanted you to hear this from me, with all my love.

With all my love,
Didi ❤️

My dearest Masi,

I’m coming… and I wanted to say hello before we meet. ❤️

For now, I’m very small and growing in Mumma’s tummy. It’s cosy here, although I have already submitted a few complaints about the menu. Mumma says I’m too small to be so demanding, but I’m not convinced.

There’s a little story behind why it took me so long to introduce myself.

Mumma and Baba waited eight years for this moment. They carried hope for a long time, and also the heartbreak of a difficult disappointment. So when I finally arrived, they felt happy, frightened and grateful all at once. Mumma is still learning to say, “There’s a baby,” without feeling afraid to believe it.

She wanted you to be one of the first people to know.

But when she tried to tell you, the words didn’t come easily. She knows your own journey has been difficult, and she was afraid that her happiness might bring you pain. She kept searching for the perfect way and the perfect moment, and then felt sad that she had remained quiet for so long.

She is sorry, Masi.

Please know that her silence was never because she loved you any less. It was because you matter so much to her, and she wanted to protect your heart while sharing something precious from hers.

So today, I’m helping her say it simply:

I’m here. I’m growing. And one day, I hope I’ll be in your arms.

I don’t know much about the world yet, but I already know that I have a Masi who knew my Mumma long before she became my Mumma. I hear you have many stories about her childhood. Please keep the funniest ones ready—and perhaps keep a few safely hidden from Mumma.

One day, I hope I can sit beside you, hold your finger and listen to those stories. I may fall asleep halfway through, but please don’t take it personally. I’ll be very new at listening.

You don’t have to reply immediately. Take all the time you need and feel whatever you feel. This little message comes with no expectations—only love, a gentle hello and a promise that I am already looking forward to knowing you.

Until we meet, please keep a little place for me in your heart.

With the tiniest hug,
Your little niece or nephew ❤️

With all our love ♡
